# GLORY Visual Audit — 2026-08-25

## Observed strengths

- The home page preserves the selected **Altın Eşik** direction: obsidian surfaces, restrained GLORY Gold, editorial hero asymmetry, technical mono labels and a verification-first information hierarchy.
- The whitepaper is legible, information-dense and carries a coherent technical-document posture.
- The administrator workspace already uses a disciplined dark editorial layout and a clear content-management hierarchy.

## Targeted improvements

- Replace the generic compact mark in public header and brand lockups with the user-supplied official GLORY wordmark while retaining a responsive, uncluttered nav.
- Make the **Gold Threshold** system more deliberate at major section transitions and in data modules, avoiding a redesign of the established layout.
- Give the `/6lory` holding route a branded lower layer so it feels intentional rather than underdeveloped.
- Use calmer editorial scale for selected secondary headings while retaining the hero’s monumental display treatment.
- Preserve current transparent and non-speculative token messaging; do not add market claims, social destinations or user-generated content.

## Verification scope

The final pass will check public home, whitepaper, 6lory and Control Room on desktop and mobile for logo clarity, contrast, overflow, interaction visibility and overall brand continuity.

## Mobile findings

The public pages preserve their core hierarchy at a 390px viewport. The opportunity is refinement rather than structural correction: the official wordmark needs a compact but legible mobile presentation, the 6lory route needs a more intentional GLORY lockup, and the Control Room’s horizontally dense tab rail should visibly communicate that additional management categories remain accessible without compromising the established desktop workflow.

## Completed refinements and checks

The supplied GLORY wordmark is now the shared public brand lockup in the homepage navigation, whitepaper header, 6lory route and footer. Its responsive crop retains the symbol, wordmark and supporting microcopy rather than reverting to the previous generic mark. The 6lory route now has a GLORY header, a planned-product signal panel and a documentation escape route. Desktop review confirms the official lockup is legible in the public header, whitepaper header and 6lory header. Mobile review confirms that the homepage, whitepaper and 6lory route keep their hierarchy without overflow; the Control Room category rail now remains contained in the 390px viewport and signals its swipe behavior.
